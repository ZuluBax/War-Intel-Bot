## 1. What the bot does

The bot detects Torn City ranked wars for a configured faction, retrieves the
opposing faction's **publicly available** roster from the Torn API, optionally
adds battle-strength **estimates** from FFScouter, and displays the result in
Discord. All battle-strength figures are estimates and are labelled as such.
They are not confirmed stats and may be wrong.
